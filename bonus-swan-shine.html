The Client & The Problem

Swan Shine Cleaning Services is a local cleaning company in San Antonio, TX. Like most service businesses their size, they were handling booking requests manually — phone calls, DMs, back-and-forth texts. Every lead required a human to respond, qualify, and schedule. That's time the owner doesn't have.

The goal: build a form that captures a lead, creates them in the CRM, sends them an instant SMS confirmation, and alerts the owner — with zero manual steps between submission and follow-up.

The Critical Mapping Fix (Don't Skip This)

GHL will default to suggesting {{contact.*}} variables when you build actions. This is wrong for webhook-triggered workflows. The contact doesn't exist yet when the SMS fires — so those variables return blank.

Always use {{inboundWebhookRequest.queryParams.*}} for any data that came in through the webhook. Here's the correct mapping:

GHL Field Wrong Variable Correct Variable
first_name{{contact.first_name}}{{inboundWebhookRequest.queryParams.first_name}}
last_name{{contact.last_name}}{{inboundWebhookRequest.queryParams.last_name}}
phone{{contact.phone}}{{inboundWebhookRequest.queryParams.phone}}
email{{contact.email}}{{inboundWebhookRequest.queryParams.email}}
address{{contact.address1}}{{inboundWebhookRequest.queryParams.address}}
service_type{{contact.service_type}}{{inboundWebhookRequest.queryParams.service_type}}
05

The Webhook Payload

The form POSTs a JSON body with all 10 fields. GHL receives this at the Inbound Webhook URL and makes every field available as a queryParams variable:

// Form submits this JSON to GHL webhook { "first_name": "Maria", "last_name": "Garcia", "phone": "(210) 555-0000", "email": "maria@email.com", "address": "1234 Elm St, San Antonio TX", "service_type": "Deep Clean", "preferred_date": "2026-04-28", "preferred_time": "Morning (8am–12pm)", "estimate": "$200 – $300", "notes": "3 bed / 2 bath, no pets" }
